01

Intro & the problem

ScreenTransition: fade in from black on the Explore page.StratX open on Explore, scrolled to the top. Face cam on. Slowly zoom in on the headline.

Hey, I’m Zuka, and this is StratX.

Quant traders build strategies that make money, but a strategy is only valuable while it’s secret. Show it, and you lose your edge. Hide it, and nobody believes you.

And investors can’t easily find or trust strategies without relying on unverified claims.

02

The solution

ScreenTransition: slow scroll down Explore.Scroll past the three steps (Find, Check, Make an offer) and a few strategy cards.

So we asked: what if quants could prove their performance and sell access to their strategy, without ever revealing it?

That’s StratX, a marketplace on Solana connecting quants who need capital with investors. Here’s how it works.

03

Getting in

ScreenTransition: quick cut to the onboarding page.Open /onboarding → click Get started straight away. Hover Google, Apple, Email, then the wallet list. Point at “StratX never asks for your seed phrase or private key.” Click Phantom → approve → choose Both → move quickly through the steps → Finish.

Sign in with Google, Apple, email, or any Solana wallet. We never ask for your private keys.

04

Discover

ScreenTransition: Finish drops you on Explore. No cut.Type: low risk strategies with a long track record → Enter. Hold on “How we understood your search”, then the “Why it matched” line on the Basalt card.

Investors search in plain English, and StratX shows how it read the search and why every result matched.

05

Reputation

ScreenTransition: click the Basalt card and let it load on camera.Hold on the Diamond badge. Scroll to “In plain words”, then “What you can see vs. what stays private”. Click the Reputation tab and open one factor.

Every strategy gets a reputation tier, from Bronze to Obsidian, scored on seven factors like risk-adjusted returns, loss control, track record and verification.

And we explain it in plain words: about eighteen percent a year, worst drop under three percent. Investors see the results. The code stays private.

06

Listing a strategy

ScreenTransition: click Create strategy in the top bar.Type a name and summary → Continue → Use example data → hold on the analysis (Silver, “self-reported”) → Continue → Review & publish → Create.

Quants list a strategy in minutes by uploading their daily account values. No code. StratX scores it instantly, and self-reported numbers stay capped until they’re verified.

07

The auction

ScreenTransition: Create opens the “Start an auction now?” pop-up by itself.Asking price 15000, lowest offer 10000 → Start auction → approve. Jump-cut: a buyer offers 12000, another offers 14000. Back as the quant: My strategies → Accept $14,000 → approve → open it in Solana Explorer.
$13,580to the quant · 97%
$420StratX · 3%

Then they start an auction. The quant sets a price, investors compete with offers, and the quant accepts the one they want.

Every offer is locked in USDC on Solana. Accept fourteen thousand, and one transaction sends ninety-seven percent to the quant and three to StratX, and everyone else can withdraw their offer.

If the program isn’t deployed when you record: show Basalt’s demo auction on the Auctions page and say “Here’s how it settles:” before “Accept fourteen thousand”. Don’t show a fake Explorer link.

Customers & market

ScreenTransition: cut back to Explore.Slow scroll through the strategy cards while you talk.

Our customers are quants who want to monetize their strategies, and investors who want proven performance.

Crypto hedge funds are a $136 billion market inside a $6 trillion hedge fund industry, yet individual quant strategies still have no dedicated marketplace.

09

Business model

ScreenTransition: click Auctions in the top bar.Point at “Paid instantly: 97% to the quant, 3% to StratX”. Then open a strategy’s Reputation tab and point at “Capital Commitment”.

We take a 3% fee on every successful auction, and 97% goes to the quant.

Quants can also back their claims with their own money, which boosts their reputation. Next, if they miss their target, that money goes to charity, not to us.

Why Solana

ScreenTransition: cut to the accept transaction in Solana Explorer.Point at the two USDC payments.

And this only works on Solana. Without it, someone has to hold every investor’s money, and you’d have to trust them. On Solana, a program holds it, and every offer costs under a cent.
